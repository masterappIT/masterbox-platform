Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

IM MASTER INC. LIMITED ("we", "us") operates MasterBox, a multi-channel inbox that lets businesses receive and reply to customer messages from Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp in one place. MasterApp is a brand of IM MASTER INC. LIMITED.

This policy explains how we handle personal data, in line with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong.

2. Our role

We are responsible for the data of businesses and team members who hold MasterBox accounts. For conversations between a business and its customers, the business decides how that data is used, and we process it on the business's behalf to provide MasterBox.

3. Information we collect

  • Business account details: company name, address, contact email, phone number, business registration details and the name of the person in charge.
  • Team member details: name, login email, phone number and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form.
  • Connected accounts: when a business connects a messaging account, we receive and store its Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp Business account identifiers, names, phone numbers and the access tokens needed to operate it.
  • Customer conversations: when someone messages a connected account, we receive and store their platform user ID or WhatsApp number, their name and profile picture where available, the message content, and delivery status and timestamps.
  • Lead ad submissions: if a business connects Meta lead ads, we receive the information people submit through those forms.
  • Technical data: server logs such as IP address and request time, which we use to run and secure the service.

4. How we use information

  • To show conversations to the business's team and send their replies.
  • To connect and maintain the accounts a business authorises.
  • To secure the service, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To provide support and tell account holders about the service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use data received from Meta platforms for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

  • Meta and other messaging platforms, to deliver messages and operate connected accounts. Their own privacy policies apply to data they hold.
  • Service providers that host and run our infrastructure, under obligations to protect the data.
  • Tools a business chooses to connect to its MasterBox account.
  • Authorities or other parties where the law requires it, or where needed to protect rights and safety.

6. Where data is stored

Our servers and those of our service providers and messaging platforms may be located outside Hong Kong, so data may be transferred to and processed in other places.

7. How long we keep it

We keep data while the business's account is active, unless it is deleted earlier on request. After an account closes, we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and hashed passwords to protect data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, and you may ask us to delete it. See Data Deletion Instructions. If you messaged a business that uses MasterBox, you may also contact that business directly, since it controls that conversation.

10. Cookies and browser storage

The MasterBox dashboard stores information in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website or the dashboard.

11. Children

MasterBox is a service for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top of this page when we do. If a change significantly affects how we handle your data, we will tell account holders.

1. 我們是誰

IM MASTER INC. LIMITED（下稱「我們」）營運 MasterBox。MasterBox 是多渠道收件匣，讓商家在同一個地方接收及回覆來自 Facebook Messenger、Instagram 和 WhatsApp 的客戶訊息。MasterApp 是 IM MASTER INC. LIMITED 的品牌。

本政策說明我們如何依照香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）處理個人資料。

2. 我們的角色

持有 MasterBox 帳戶的商家及團隊成員的資料，由我們負責。至於商家與其客戶之間的對話，由商家決定資料的用途，我們則代商家處理這些資料，以提供 MasterBox 服務。

3. 我們收集的資料

  • 商家帳戶資料：公司名稱、地址、聯絡電郵、電話號碼、商業登記資料及負責人姓名。
  • 團隊成員資料：姓名、登入電郵、電話號碼及密碼。密碼以雜湊方式儲存。
  • 已連接的帳戶：商家連接訊息帳戶時，我們會接收並儲存其 Facebook 專頁、Instagram 帳戶或 WhatsApp Business 帳戶的識別碼、名稱、電話號碼，以及操作該帳戶所需的存取權杖。
  • 客戶對話：有人傳訊息到已連接的帳戶時，我們會接收並儲存對方的平台用戶 ID 或 WhatsApp 號碼、姓名及大頭貼（如有）、訊息內容、傳送狀態和時間。
  • 潛在客戶廣告資料：如商家連接 Meta 潛在客戶廣告，我們會接收用戶透過這些表格提交的資料。
  • 技術資料：IP 位址和請求時間等伺服器記錄，用於運作及保護服務。

4. 我們如何使用資料

  • 向商家團隊顯示對話，並傳送他們的回覆。
  • 連接及維護商家授權的帳戶。
  • 保護服務安全、防止濫用及修復問題。
  • 提供支援，並向帳戶持有人發出服務相關通知。
  • 履行法律責任。

我們不會出售個人資料，也不會把從 Meta 平台取得的資料用於廣告。

5. 我們會與誰分享資料

  • Meta 及其他訊息平台：用於傳送訊息及操作已連接的帳戶。這些平台持有的資料受其各自的私隱政策約束。
  • 服務供應商：負責託管及運作我們基礎設施的供應商，他們有責任保護資料。
  • 商家選擇連接的工具：商家自行連接到 MasterBox 帳戶的工具。
  • 有關當局或其他方：在法律要求，或為保護權利及安全而有需要時。

6. 資料儲存地點

我們、服務供應商及訊息平台的伺服器可能位於香港以外，因此資料可能會傳送到其他地方並在當地處理。

7. 資料保留期限

商家帳戶有效期間，我們會保留資料，除非已按要求提早刪除。帳戶結束後，我們會在合理期間內刪除個人資料或將其匿名化，但法律要求保留更久的除外。

8. 資料安全

我們使用加密連線（HTTPS）、存取控制及雜湊密碼保護資料。任何系統都無法做到絕對安全，因此我們無法保證資料絕對安全。

9. 你的權利

你可以要求查閱或更正你的個人資料，也可以要求我們刪除。詳情請參閱資料刪除說明。如果你曾傳訊息給使用 MasterBox 的商家，也可以直接聯絡該商家，因為該對話由商家控制。

10. Cookie 及瀏覽器儲存

MasterBox 管理後台會在你的瀏覽器儲存資料，讓你保持登入並記住你的設定。本網站及管理後台均不使用廣告或分析 Cookie。

11. 兒童

MasterBox 是為商家而設的服務，並非以 18 歲以下兒童為對象。

12. 政策更新

我們可能更新本政策，並會同時更改本頁頂部的日期。如更新對資料處理方式有重大影響，我們會通知帳戶持有人。
