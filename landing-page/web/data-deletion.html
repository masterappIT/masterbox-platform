Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 5 November 2026

閱讀繁體中文版

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Email info@masterappinc.com with the subject "MasterBox data deletion request".
  2. Tell us which data you want deleted, and include the details that help us find it:
    • Business account holders: your company name and login email.
    • Connected accounts: the Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number.
    • People who messaged a business: the business you messaged, the platform you used, and your name on that platform or your WhatsApp number.
  3. We may ask you to confirm that the data is yours before we delete it.

What happens next

We will complete your request within 30 days and confirm by email. We delete the relevant data from MasterBox, such as your account details, access tokens, conversations and profile information.

We may keep information the law requires us to keep, and will tell you if this applies. If you messaged a business, we may also let that business know about your request, since it controls that conversation.

Removing MasterBox from Facebook

You can also stop MasterBox's access in Facebook: go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Apps and Websites, and remove MasterBox. This stops future access, but does not delete data we already hold. To delete that data, please also email us as described above.

資料刪除說明

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

Read in English

如何要求刪除你的資料

  1. 電郵至 info@masterappinc.com，主旨請寫「MasterBox data deletion request」。
  2. 告訴我們你要刪除哪些資料，並提供有助我們找到資料的內容：
    • 商家帳戶持有人：公司名稱及登入電郵。
    • 已連接的帳戶：Facebook 專頁、Instagram 帳戶或 WhatsApp 號碼。
    • 曾傳訊息給商家的人士：你傳訊息的商家、使用的平台，以及你在該平台的名稱或 WhatsApp 號碼。
  3. 刪除前，我們可能會請你確認資料屬於你。

之後的處理

我們會在 30 天內完成你的要求，並以電郵確認。我們會從 MasterBox 刪除相關資料，例如帳戶資料、存取權杖、對話及個人檔案資料。

法律要求保留的資料，我們可能會繼續保留，如有這種情況會通知你。如果你曾傳訊息給商家，由於該對話由商家控制，我們也可能會把你的要求告知該商家。

從 Facebook 移除 MasterBox

你也可以在 Facebook 停止 MasterBox 的存取權限：前往「設定和私隱」、「設定」、「應用程式和網站」，然後移除 MasterBox。這樣只會停止之後的存取，不會刪除我們已持有的資料。如要刪除這些資料，請同時按上述方式電郵給我們。
